# Contributing to Wer

Wer is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or
later, written and maintained by Hudco Lighting LLC. Bug reports are welcome
at any time. Patches are merged only under the agreement below, and only
after asking, because most will not be.

## Problems and bug reports

Send them to **wer@hudco.lighting** with `wer.log` attached (**Help → Open
Log Folder**), what you were doing, and the version and build from the
**Environment** tab. There is no issue tracker; that address is read by the
person who wrote Wer.

## Patches

Hudco Lighting holds the copyright in Wer as a whole and means to keep the
right to license it in other ways later. A merged patch would give its author
a share of that copyright, so a pull request is merged only after its author
has agreed to the Contributor Licence Agreement below, once, by email. Ask before starting anything larger
than a fix: Wer is one person's tool for one job, and a change that suits a
different job may not be taken however good it is.
